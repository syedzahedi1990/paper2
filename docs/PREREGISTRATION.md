## H1: transfer, trace production and trace use

The primary no-trace estimand is trained-minus-base ordinary-target accuracy on the **seven unrewarded** views under immediate answering. Average views and cue replicas within seed and story, then the three fixed seeds within story. Report direct accuracy separately and retain per-view and oracle-derived scope splits.

For each reader r and trace source s, report B(r,s) = repaired no-trace errors / that reader's no-trace errors. Use the full crossed base/trained 2×2 panel on identical stories, questions and saved text. Report source contrasts B(r,trained)−B(r,base), reader contrasts B(trained,s)−B(base,s), their interaction, introduced errors and net accuracy changes. A ceiling reader has an undefined repair fraction, not zero. Keep question-dependent and frozen-direct timings separate.

The edit axis is the increase, relative to the original trace, in responses matching the edited oracle forecast on questions whose forecast changes. “Changes” compares the oracle forecast under the trace-stated premise before and after the edit, including when the original stated belief is wrong; ordinary accuracy still uses the true task answer. Primary: minimal edits of the focal belief actually stated in the trained source's `direct_fixed` trace, read by the corresponding trained reader. Report regenerated edits alongside it, preservation on intact forecasts, and the full crossed controls. Plot this axis against unrewarded no-trace transfer as points with both intervals; candidate quadrant readings are interpretations, never automatic categories. The oracle premise control supplies only the queried belief, not the whole state. Answer cue and queried-belief premise are the Test 2 positive controls. Null edit sensitivity is interpreted against these, replay, paraphrase and irrelevant edits; resistance to a false premise is a possible finding.

## H2: cue dependence and scope

Report separately: no-trace improvement on unrewarded belief-dependent views; intact-view performance and retention of previously correct answers; per-question agree/independent/conflict accuracies and contrasts; and joint correctness of all eight questions for each literal cue rendering. Dependence labels come from the per-world oracle. Cue agreement is defined against the rewarded direct answer, so never pool its contrast across question types. Average replicas within story/view; retain paired stories across conditions. Compare clean and shortcut arms both at final checkpoints and under the development-only matching rule below.

## H3: within-checkpoint component effects

Primary mask: **every layer from 1 through the final layer**, excluding layer 0, resolved separately for each architecture. No activation patch precedes it. Swap critical-token pre-RoPE keys, values and residual outputs within each checkpoint, with complete own, signed-permutation and natural-donor panels on the identical mask. Per-layer results are descriptive; cross-model swaps are secondary and require identical token inputs.

Score changed forecasts, intact forecasts and the complete eight-view event-rewrite pattern separately. For each component, report its natural-minus-own effect within checkpoint and the trained-minus-base change in that effect, for forecast probabilities and consequence-pattern correctness. Report signed-null contrasts alongside them. The same-band residual intervention is the positive control. Its estimate, interval and nulls inform interpretation; no automatic positive-effect test passes G4. A null key/value estimate without informative positive-control evidence remains inconclusive. Other Paper 1 forecast accounts are diagnostics, not alternative primary targets chosen after results.

For the revised proposal’s descriptive cross-arm prediction, plot all eight arm-by-training-dataset combinations separately for K, V and residual. Use seven-unrewarded-view immediate accuracy transfer on the horizontal axis and the trained-minus-base change in natural-minus-own joint event-rewrite correctness on the vertical axis, both on the same clean confirmation stories. Show both whole-story intervals after averaging three seeds within story. Keep final and matched-checkpoint panels separate. No regression, correlation significance test or data-selected metric is used; see the execution amendment for the frozen display estimand.

## Matching, intervals and editing uncertainty

Preregister the compared arm/dataset/seed run identities. On saved **immediate/direct/development** checkpoints, compute each run's peak accuracy; the common target is the minimum of those peaks. Select each run's checkpoint closest to that target; ties use the earlier step, then checkpoint ID. Report achieved accuracies and signed gaps. No tolerance, interpolation or outcome-based exclusion. A trained run's step-zero checkpoint is eligible; the separate untrained base is the reference, not a training trajectory limiting the target. RL retains immediate checkpoints every 50 steps and native traces every 250; include step zero and final steps.

Use **20,000 paired whole-story bootstrap resamples**, seed 2026, percentile 95% intervals. Average fixed training seeds inside each story before resampling; ratio estimands resample story-level numerators and denominators together. These intervals generalize over stories, not a population of training seeds. Report counts, undefined denominators and usable bootstrap draws. Primary analyses are the declared contrasts; secondary per-layer/per-view exploration is descriptive. No significance or interval-width cutoff selects results.

Freeze the editor model/revision, prompt, decoding and random audit sample before generation. Use all 120 candidates and preserve noneditability and operational failure outcomes. Editability, reasons and conditional edit effects receive per-arm story intervals; report common-support comparisons and unequal eligible populations. Count each source artifact once despite its eight questions and crossed readers; average cue renderings within source seed and fixed source seeds within story. Retain source-checkpoint descriptions alongside the pooled source-arm/dataset report. The default uniform audit samples 100 contracts, with source-coverage supplements reported separately; this is a workload choice, not a pass mark. Report exact finite-population flaw-rate intervals, including original flaws after correction. Global rates are not per-arm guarantees. Use source-specific simultaneous upper bounds and actual story/seed/view weights for worst-case effect-bias sensitivity; a contrast in [−1,1] can shift by twice the flawed weight. All human-modified controls receive their full possible weight conservatively, because the audit sampled their original machine outputs. Keep this sensitivity analysis distinct from bootstrap uncertainty: the plot draws bootstrap intervals and retains the audit bias cap and clipped sensitivity range in its numeric sidecar. Missing audit bounds are unavailable, never zero. Details and correction provenance are in [EDIT_GENERATION.md](EDIT_GENERATION.md).

## Pilot decisions and evidence

Each G0–G4 decision requires a named reviewer, rationale and immutable evidence. Integrity checks remain mandatory; empirical magnitudes have no numerical pass marks.

| Gate | Evidence presented to its reviewer |
|---|---|
| G0 | Base direct development accuracy and interval relative to chance (1/6) and ceiling; failures by depth/event; all heuristic accuracies and intervals by stratum; model-versus-heuristic reference figure; chosen difficulty and frozen corpus. Judge whether the base is clearly above chance and below ceiling. |
| G1 | Native and immediate learning curves, with step-zero and final measurements, for **reasoning RL, answer-only RL and label-only SFT** on clean 3B/seed-1 data. Bind completed run artifacts and the common development panel by hash. Record a separate written interpretation for each arm, considering uncertainty, stability, truncation, exposures, optimization and whether a weak arm draws matching toward step zero. Retain all selection-panel candidate results. Flat curves do not trigger a numerical rule; the reviewer judges whether the comparison is informative before paid runs. |
| G2 | Exact designed cue rates, length/entity matching, real-tokenizer checks, disjoint story splits and frozen provenance. |
| G3 | Raw and re-rendered own-trace replay; audited editor examples, flaw intervals, editability/reason intervals; answer-cue and queried-belief positive controls; paraphrase/irrelevant controls and both edit continuations. Judge the measurement procedure, not whether a preferred edit effect appears. |
| G4 | Native implementation receipts; complete all-1-to-last K/V/residual panels; residual changed/intact/joint estimates and intervals, own and signed nulls. Judge sensitivity and useful precision in context. |

After these pilot reviews, confirmation reports the measurements and limitations without reapplying invented thresholds. Preserve complete panels, exact span/entity/length matching where specified, no leakage, frozen hashes, and cost authorization. Official OpenToM prompts and subtype macro-F1 with whole-story intervals are primary externally; adapted prompts are secondary. Zhong reports a different accuracy aggregation, so this is not a verified reproduction of its numerical scores. No null establishes absence of a stored belief, and no quadrant point identifies where a gain is stored.
