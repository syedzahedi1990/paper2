# Design changes to disclose in Paper 2

These are implementation choices and limitations relative to the supplied proposal, not empirical findings. The original proposal remains unchanged in `source_proposal.docx`. [PREREGISTRATION.md](PREREGISTRATION.md) fixes H1–H3 estimands and analyses before training. Version 0.6 removes invented statistical pass marks: G0–G4 are named pilot reviews of measurement validity, followed by confirmatory estimation and reporting.

## World and story distribution

Use **“Paper 1's world, new story distribution.”** The byte-identical epistemic state engine, two characters, vocabulary and forecast semantics are retained. Stories differ: the default has nine focal-object events, varied observers and a deciding observation among six interior positions. Depth-two stories use preregistered joint quotas for whether the nested answer equals both, either or neither character's own belief. These changes removed the original decisive lookup shortcut. Eight-question scores therefore are not directly comparable with Paper 1's original panel.

Weak position, location-frequency and depth-one last-joint-observation heuristics remain. Audit v5 reports every rule by split, depth and event kind, with intervals and model-comparison reference lines. Statistical screening bounds and automatic chance tests have been removed. Exact planned quotas, split separation and file integrity are still checked. Depth-two last-joint-observation is an intended semantic baseline, not a shortcut that must fail. The G0 reviewer interprets these results alongside the base model's development interval relative to chance and ceiling; the software does not decide that interval is sufficiently far from either.

## Training and compute

Only story exposure is matched: one pass over 2,000 stories. RL uses 2,000 optimizer steps and eight completions per story; SFT uses 250 steps with effective batch eight. Tokens, update counts and compute differ. The reference learning rates are transcribed from [Zhong et al.'s Appendix E](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.09092v1): RL 2e-5 and SFT 1e-6, with LLM KL coefficient zero. Their LoRA versus full-fine-tuning parameterization is unspecified; their rates are not treated as validated LoRA optima. The preregistered selection panel now spans ×1, ×3, ×10 and ×30 those rates with the same selection rule, and G1 reviews every pilot arm’s learning curve. Their two epochs and four-device setup are not replicated. The proposal's LoRA modules, group size eight and 384-token cap remain fixed. [Training notes](TRAINING.md) distinguish inherited settings from implementation choices and specify an optional costed development-selection panel before pilot training.

RL retains 41 immediate-answer checkpoints (steps 0, 50, …, 2,000) and runs nine native-generation panels (0, 250, …, 2,000). At 120 development stories this entails 4,920 immediate forward passes and 1,080 sequential native generations per RL run. SFT keeps both formats every 25 steps. The pilot retains 93 evaluated adapters and the 24-run grid 624, plus final adapters and rotating optimizer checkpoints. The proposal's preliminary 15–25-hour pilot budget remains unverified. Profile both evaluation schedules, training, storage, editing, continuation regeneration and Tests 1–3 against the fixed £500 ceiling.

Development matching now targets the highest accuracy every compared run reaches: the minimum of their maximum development direct accuracies. Select each run's closest retained checkpoint and report actual accuracies and target deviations. There is no tolerance cutoff, interpolated adapter or confirmation-based selection. Unconditional and matched comparisons are both reported; matching does not remove every optimization confound.

## Trace interventions

The primary confirmatory edit measurement uses the native direct-question trace, frozen across the eight consequence views. Question-dependent edits remain an additional timing analysis when compute permits, with separate artifacts. The primary uses all 120 candidate confirmation stories; 24 trained final checkpoints plus one shared base and a single rendering entail 3,000 candidate contracts, versus 24,000 question-dependent contracts. Additional matched checkpoints and renderings increase these counts.

One editor model, immutable revision and prompt generates paraphrases, irrelevant controls and the targeted change to the focal belief actually stated, including incorrect beliefs. Oracle forecasts distinguish stated-before, stated-after and world-truth answers. Missing, ambiguous or contradicted beliefs, unsafe controls and generation failures stay reported outcomes. They do not invalidate the entire batch or disappear from candidate denominators. Existing answer statements may be preserved; newly introduced answer-label leakage is rejected. Source continuations are generated once and shared by both readers. Model-generated controls follow the broad precedent of Lanham et al.; our contracts, irrelevant-edit construction, classifications and audit design are study-specific.

A preregistered random human sample checks editor quality, with source/checkpoint coverage supplements. Original flaws, corrections and re-review remain visible. G3 validates the editing procedure during the pilot. Confirmation reports the flaw estimate and interval without a maximum-failure acceptance rule. Missing judgments, altered hashes and known invalid uncorrected controls remain integrity failures. The flaw rate constrains how far defective controls could shift the edit-following rate, using the correct eligible-control denominator; a global candidate bound is not a per-arm assurance. See [the edit protocol](EDIT_GENERATION.md).

Report editability by arm/source and conditional edit effects with intervals, averaging fixed seeds within each story before 20,000 whole-story bootstrap resamples. Also report paired common support: separate eligible populations can reflect differences in which traces state an editable belief. There is no required 97-story count or ±10-point interval, and imprecise outcomes are still reported. Plot every arm as a point with intervals on both axes. The proposal's quadrant names are candidate interpretations, not automatically assigned classes.

The belief-summary control now supplies **only the queried belief**, as a premise for later consequence questions. It remains an oracle positive control, not a model-produced summary. The former whole-state summary has been removed. Test 2 nulls are interpreted alongside this premise and the answer-cue positive control.

## Component positive controls

Primary Test 3 swaps every layer **from 1 through the final model layer**, within each checkpoint: 1–35 for the pinned 3B architecture and 1–27 for 7B. K, V and residual swaps use the same joint band; individual-layer results are descriptive. Freeze `g4_layer_policy="all_after_embedding_layer"` alongside the pilot evidence list; the policy resolves the correct band for each model architecture. Paper 1's block-4 activation patch does not occur here, so its later band start is not imported. Natural donor, own and signed-permutation displacement controls remain mandatory.

G4 verifies complete structurally aligned panels and records residual/null estimates and intervals. The reviewer judges sensitivity; a positive point estimate is no longer an automatic requirement. The residual hook occurs after each block's own K/V computation, so all-layer residual replacement need not reproduce donor logits. Custom or full-depth masks remain secondary diagnostics, and narrow-layer nulls cannot establish absence of a distributed effect. Read key/value nulls against the same-band residual positive control and permutation nulls.

## External benchmark and answer format

Official OpenToM system/user prompt text and lexical scoring now supply the primary external macro-F1 by subtype, with 20,000 whole-narrative intervals and explicit invalid-output exclusions. The Paper 2 adapted prompt/scorer remains secondary. Qwen chat serialization, completion caps and full normal-length corpus selection are still disclosed implementation choices. Zhong et al. describe accuracy and macro averages by order, and their exact prompts/splits were not available in their README-only code release; this is not an exact replication of their published numbers. See [OpenToM details](OPENTOM.md).

Controlled-world answers use inventory letters A–F rather than Paper 1's location words. Both are single Qwen tokens under the audited vocabulary. Report the label mapping and single-token check; this optional format departure is retained.

## Historical implementations

Versions 0.3–0.5 introduced heuristic screening bounds, matching tolerance ±0.02, an editable-story/interval-width criterion, numerical edit-audit acceptance and a positive residual point-estimate check. Those historical rules are recorded in the correction history for provenance; **none is part of the v0.6 analysis or pilot decision rule**. Old calibration and edit/audit lineages must not silently inherit the revised protocol. Frozen hashes, complete panels and the named reviewers' actual decisions remain required.
