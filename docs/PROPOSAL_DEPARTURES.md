# Design changes to disclose in Paper 2

These are implementation choices and limitations relative to the supplied proposal, not empirical findings. The original proposal remains unchanged in `source_proposal.docx`.

## World and story distribution

Use the description **“Paper 1's world, new story distribution.”** Paper 2 retains the byte-identical epistemic state engine, two characters, object/location vocabulary and forecast semantics. It does not reuse Paper 1's stories. The revised default has nine focal-object events, varied observers and a deciding observation among six interior positions. Depth-two stories follow fixed, preregistered joint quotas for whether the nested answer equals both, either or neither character's own belief. The eight-question results therefore are not directly comparable with Paper 1's eight-question panel. Report distributions and within-Paper-2 comparisons explicitly.

The decisive first-event/last-event/initial-location shortcuts were removed, but the distribution still supports weak position, location-frequency and depth-one last-joint-observation heuristics. Audit v4 scores these separately by split, depth and critical-event kind; the scores are retained even when they fail screening. The current distribution is intentionally retained rather than repeatedly redesigned until an audit looks favourable. G0 requires an explicit corpus-bound decision about these remaining risks. This is acceptance of a limitation, not a declaration that every shortcut is at chance. At depth two, the last jointly observed focal event implements the intended nested-belief update in this world; it is reported as a semantic baseline, not required to fail as a shortcut.

## Training and compute

Only story exposure is matched. SFT uses the provisional learning rate 1e-4 and 250 optimizer steps per full pool; RL uses 1e-5 and 2,000 steps, with eight completions per story. Tokens, optimizer steps and compute differ. Report unconditional results together with development-selected matched-immediate-accuracy results and missing matches. Matching does not remove every optimization confound.

RL immediate-answer evaluation and adapter retention occur at steps 0, 50, …, 2,000 (41 checkpoints); native generation remains at 0, 250, …, 2,000 (nine panels). At 120 development worlds this is 4,920 immediate forward passes and 1,080 sequential native generations per RL run. SFT keeps both formats every 25 steps. The denser immediate schedule improves matching resolution but does not guarantee a checkpoint within ±0.02. Report missing matches without interpolation. The default pilot retains 93 evaluated adapters, and the 24-run confirmatory grid retains 624, before separate final adapters and rotating trainer checkpoints.

The proposal's 15–25-hour whole-pilot estimate remains unverified. Profile native and immediate evaluation separately, along with training, saves and storage; account for all arms and subsequent experiments, including edit generation, source regeneration and crossed reading. Old profiles without separate evaluation timings cannot supply a complete projection. See [training accounting](TRAINING.md).
## Trace interventions and precision

Twelve edited worlds remain a pilot diagnostic. Confirmatory preparation now defaults to all 120 candidate worlds, with **97 eligible independent worlds per arm/checkpoint** required for the prespecified 95% interval half-width of .10 on a story-averaged rate. The count is a worst-case-variance normal planning approximation, not a coverage guarantee or a plan for arm differences or ratios. Actual eligible counts and story-cluster intervals must also meet the criterion. Changed/intact subgroups and paired common support may be smaller and fail. Do not assign a quadrant from an imprecise axis or an unplanned effect threshold. More candidate worlds do not guarantee editability or adequate precision.

The primary confirmatory edit measurement uses only the native direct-question trace frozen across all eight consequence views. Question-dependent edits remain pilot or supplemental diagnostics. At 120 candidate worlds, 24 trained final checkpoints plus a shared base and one clean rendering require **3,000 contracts rather than 24,000**; additional matched checkpoints and renderings add contracts.

The semantic intervention changes the focal belief actually stated in the trace, even when it is wrong. It no longer requires a span asserting the world's correct focal belief. The oracle computes before/after consequences for that stated belief, keeping world-truth labels separate. The frozen destination differs from both the stated belief and the true focal belief: it uses the original scope-edit target unless that would be a no-op, when a deterministic alternative is selected. Missing, ambiguous or contradicted beliefs and the absence of safe controls are reported noneditable outcomes. The audited classification permits these outcomes without failing every other contract. Noneditable candidates receive no targeted edit controls, while their ordinary measurements remain available. Malformed, truncated and failed editor outputs remain distinct `generation_failed` outcomes without usable controls, rather than automatically invalidating the whole batch. Their original rows, denominator contributions and error records remain; provenance tampering, an incomplete batch and missing human judgments still block use. Existing source answer statements are allowed; new or changed answer-label leakage is rejected.

One fixed editor model, immutable revision and preregistered prompt generates candidate controls. A preregistered random human sample checks control validity and noneditability classifications. Audit v2 defaults to 100 global candidate contracts plus source/checkpoint coverage, permits at most one original sampled flaw, and requires an exact finite-population one-sided 95% upper error bound of at most 5% over all candidate contracts. Human correction and re-review of sampled flaws bind the effective controls without erasing the original error count or raw model batch. No adaptive model resampling is allowed. The eligible-subset error bound is reported separately; neither the global bound nor one coverage item per source establishes a per-arm 5% guarantee. Unsampled controls remain individually unapproved.

Report per-arm/source editability rates, reasons and eligible denominators alongside conditional effect estimates. Comparisons over separate eligible populations can reflect selection differences, including correctness-dependent editability; report paired common-support comparisons separately. Neither conditional estimate describes every candidate world. The editor still needs pilot validation, and selected counts are not empirical precision. These v0.5 choices require a new frozen protocol lineage.

Model-generated controls follow the broad precedent of Lanham et al.; our stated-belief contracts, irrelevant edits, noneditability outcomes and tolerated-error audit are study-specific choices. See [the exact workflow and limitations](EDIT_GENERATION.md). Alternative manually chosen facts require explicit reviewed forecasts.
The belief-summary condition supplies the full relevant symbolic state. Its prose directly gives the answer for many questions. Interpret it as a positive control for use of a correct premise, not as evidence about the model's own summary. The second timing condition reuses the native direct-question trace across later questions; it does not introduce an untrained story-description prompt.

## Component positive controls

Full-depth K/V swaps remain available. The runbook illustrates broad early-interior-to-last bands (6–35 for the pinned 36-block 3B model; 6–27 for the pinned 28-block 7B model). Those start points require calibration and preregistration, and are not validated architecture-independent thresholds. A narrow-band null cannot support a general absence-of-effect claim. G4 requires an explicitly preregistered interior **tested band** with matching simultaneous K, V and residual masks; the residual starts and ends at the same layers as the key/value band whose sensitivity it supports. Declare it through `--g4-test-layers` and freeze the identical `g4_test_layers` in calibration settings. Merely starting at layer 1 or selecting an unrelated interior residual does not qualify. Layer-zero bands and final-block-only residuals remain ineligible. A passing control for this band does not automatically validate a null full-depth diagnostic.

G4 checks complete matching natural, own and null panels and a positive changed-consequence residual effect, while the investigator reviews preservation, flat nulls and useful precision. The hook replaces block outputs after that block's own keys and values have been computed; all-layer residual replacement therefore need not reproduce donor logits. Excluding layer zero is a conservative safeguard, not an assertion of exact donor equivalence.

These scores are not directly comparable with published OpenToM numbers, including Zhong et al.'s, without a separately matched protocol. See [the external-evaluation specification](OPENTOM.md).
